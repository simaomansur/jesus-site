Clinical Indication

Chronic Refractory Osteomyelitis

Hyperbaric oxygen therapy may be considered as an adjunct for selected cases of persistent bone infection that have not responded adequately to conventional approaches such as antibiotics and surgical treatment when appropriate.

Request an Evaluation

Where HBOT may fit into treatment

Appropriateness depends on the medical record, prescribing physician, clinical circumstances, and any applicable insurance requirements. No specific result can be guaranteed.

Information useful for referral

Infectious disease and surgical notes, imaging, cultures, antibiotic history, and documentation of the persistent or recurrent infection.
