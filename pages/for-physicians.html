Partnering With Referring Practices

Rocky Mountain Hyperbaric Institute works with physicians and healthcare providers throughout Colorado to provide hyperbaric oxygen therapy for patients with a range of medical conditions.

Since 2007, we have provided hyperbaric treatment using hospital-grade chambers, established medical protocols, and certified hyperbaric technicians. Our team works directly with referring offices to coordinate patient evaluations, insurance authorization, and treatment scheduling.

Our Referral Process

Designed to reduce administrative back-and-forth between referring offices and our team.

01

Submit Patient Records

Complete our referral form and provide relevant medical records, including the diagnosis, clinical notes, and supporting diagnostic studies. Referrals may be faxed to our office.

02

Clinical Review & Insurance Authorization

Our team reviews the submitted documentation. Our insurance team manages benefits verification, medical documentation requirements, and prior authorization directly with the patient’s insurance provider.

03

Treatment Coordination

Once the patient has been evaluated and cleared for treatment, we coordinate scheduling and provide HBOT according to the prescribed protocol. We can communicate with the referring office as appropriate.

Referral Documentation

To help avoid delays in reviewing your patient’s referral, please submit:

  • Completed physician referral form
  • Patient demographic sheet and insurance information
  • Recent clinical notes supporting the diagnosis
  • Relevant laboratory results, imaging, audiograms, or diagnostic studies

Additional documentation may be requested depending on the diagnosis and insurance requirements. Referring offices can download our referral form and return it with supporting documentation by fax.
