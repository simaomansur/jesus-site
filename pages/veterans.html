Veterans & Hyperbaric Medicine

Helping Veterans Access Hyperbaric Care

We work with the Rocky Mountain Hyperbaric Association for Brain Injuries to connect veterans with support for accessing treatment.

Our Work With Veterans

RMHI has worked with veterans affected by traumatic brain injury and other service-related health challenges. Our clinical team reviews each patient’s medical history, evaluates whether HBOT may be appropriate, and provides treatment according to the individual’s prescribed protocol.

HBOT for traumatic brain injury and PTSD remains investigational. It is considered a supplemental option, not a replacement for established medical or mental health care.

For assistance with veteran treatment expenses, program eligibility, or travel arrangements, the Rocky Mountain Hyperbaric Association for Brain Injuries is the best place to start.

Our nonprofit partner

Healing Our Heroes

The Association is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization supporting eligible post-9/11 veterans with combat-related brain injuries by helping with hyperbaric treatment costs and lodging for those traveling to Louisville, Colorado.

The Association manages program information, assistance requests, and applications. Eligibility and availability are determined by the nonprofit.
