Additional Clinical Applications

Other Uses of Hyperbaric Oxygen Therapy

Some patients ask about applications beyond recognized clinical indications. These require a careful discussion of the available evidence and coverage limitations.

Individual Review, Clear Expectations

Research continues in areas such as neurologic injury and recovery. Evidence of benefit is not established for every proposed use, and many applications may be considered investigational or off-label.

RMHI does not represent these applications as equivalent to recognized indications or imply that insurance covers them. Specific diagnoses and summaries will be added following clinical review.
