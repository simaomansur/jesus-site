Pricing & Coverage

Clear expectations, explained simply

Costs and coverage for hyperbaric oxygen therapy vary by individual. We’ll help you understand the process and what questions to ask before you commit.

How pricing works

Hyperbaric therapy isn’t one-size-fits-all, and pricing depends on medical context and the plan recommended.

The overall cost of care can depend on factors like the type of condition being evaluated, the number of sessions recommended, and whether treatment is part of a broader medical care plan.

We aim to be transparent early. If you’re exploring HBOT, we’ll talk through common cost drivers, what a typical course of care can involve, and what next steps look like.

Insurance and coverage

Coverage varies widely by insurer, plan, and diagnosis. We encourage patients to verify benefits directly with their insurance provider.

Coverage varies by condition

Some medical indications may be eligible for coverage, while others may not. Coverage decisions are ultimately determined by insurers and individual plans.

Plan details matter

Deductibles, prior authorization requirements, and visit limits can all affect out-of-pocket cost. Asking the right questions up front makes the process smoother.

Clear communication

If helpful, we can explain what information is commonly requested and what to ask when verifying benefits, so you can make an informed decision.

What to expect financially

Our goal is to keep the process straightforward and calm.

Clear discussion before treatment Questions encouraged Individualized recommendations No pressure to proceed

Final recommendations and costs are determined after individual review.
