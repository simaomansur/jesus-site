About Rocky Mountain Hyperbaric Institute

Hyperbaric Medicine Has Been Our Focus Since 2007

Rocky Mountain Hyperbaric Institute has served Colorado patients and referring medical providers since 2007. Hyperbaric oxygen therapy is our specialty, and it has been from the beginning.

About RMHI

For nearly two decades, we’ve provided hyperbaric oxygen therapy to patients with a range of medical conditions. Our work centers on hospital-grade hyperbaric chambers, established treatment protocols, and a team that knows this specialty.

Our Clinical Team

Our clinical team includes certified hyperbaric technicians, with treatment provided under the oversight of our medical director. Staff qualifications include specialized certifications through the National Board of Diving & Hyperbaric Medical Technology (NBDHMT) and the American Board of Wound Healing (ABWH).

Our Reputation

We’ve worked with patients and referring physicians throughout Colorado for years. We value those relationships and take the practical details seriously: clear communication, careful treatment coordination, and helping patients navigate insurance authorization when applicable.

Questions about RMHI?

Our team can help with treatment questions, insurance coordination, and physician referrals.
