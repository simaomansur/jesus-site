Insurance & Medicare

Medicare & Major Insurance Plans Accepted

Our insurance team coordinates verification and authorization so you can understand your options before treatment.

Insurance Coordination, From Start to Finish

Our dedicated insurance team manages every step of the verification and authorization process, from reviewing medical records and confirming benefits to coordinating directly with your insurance provider.

Coverage is based on your diagnosis, medical necessity, and individual insurance plan. We strive to make the process seamless while helping you understand your benefits and anticipated out-of-pocket costs before treatment begins.

Ask About Your Coverage

What our team can coordinate

  • Review of clinical documentation
  • Insurance eligibility and benefits verification
  • Prior authorization submission when required
  • Communication regarding next steps and anticipated patient responsibility

Approval is determined by the insurer and does not guarantee payment.

How the insurance review works

We work with your medical records and your plan’s requirements to determine the next steps.

01

Share your records and coverage information

We review the referral, diagnosis, medical records, and insurance details available for your case.

02

Verification and authorization

Our insurance team coordinates directly with the payer and submits prior authorization requests when required.

03

Coverage and next steps

We discuss the outcome, relevant coverage requirements, and estimated out-of-pocket costs before scheduling treatment.

We accept Medicare and work with major insurers, but acceptance does not mean every plan is in network or every diagnosis is covered. Benefits and authorization are subject to payer rules.
