Clinical Indications

Medical Conditions We Treat

Hyperbaric oxygen therapy (HBOT) is an established medical treatment used to support healing and recovery in select conditions. At Rocky Mountain Hyperbaric Institute, we provide hyperbaric medicine for patients with a variety of medical needs, including:

Diabetic Foot Ulcers & Select Non-Healing Wounds

Selected diabetic foot ulcers and other qualifying problem wounds may be evaluated for HBOT as an adjunct to comprehensive wound care.

Learn more

Delayed Radiation Tissue Injury

HBOT may be considered for certain late effects of radiation therapy involving soft tissue or bone, including radiation-related injury to the bladder or rectum.

Learn more

Compromised Skin Grafts & Flaps

HBOT may be considered when a graft or flap has compromised blood flow or is otherwise threatened.

Learn more

Chronic Refractory Osteomyelitis

Persistent bone infections that have not adequately responded to standard treatment may be evaluated for adjunctive HBOT.

Learn more

Sudden Sensorineural Hearing Loss (SSNHL)

Idiopathic sudden sensorineural hearing loss is time-sensitive and warrants prompt evaluation by an ENT or other qualified clinician.

Learn more

Treatment eligibility depends on your diagnosis, medical history, and clinical evaluation. Our team can assist with reviewing records and exploring insurance coverage when applicable.
